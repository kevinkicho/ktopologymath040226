Stellar Fusion
1.00x
Energy (MeV)—
The pp-chain dominates in Sun-like stars (T~15 MK). The CNO cycle dominates in stars >1.3 M☉. Both convert 4 protons into ⁴He + 2e⁺ + 2νₑ + 26.73 MeV.
pp-Chain
$$p+p\to^2\text{H}+e^++\nu_e$$
$$^2\text{H}+p\to^3\text{He}+\gamma$$
$$^3\text{He}+^3\text{He}\to^4\text{He}+2p$$
CNO Cycle
$$^{12}\text{C}+p\to^{13}\text{N}+\gamma$$
The CNO cycle is temperature-sensitive: rate ∝ T¹⁸ at solar core. The pp-chain rate ∝ T⁴. This steep T-dependence makes CNO dominant in massive stars.

Reaction
1.00x
⟨σv⟩ (m³/s)—
The Gamow peak is the overlap of the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution and the quantum tunneling probability. D-T peaks at ~64 keV with cross-section ~5 barns.
Reactivity ⟨σv⟩
$$\langle\sigma v\rangle = \int_0^\infty \sigma(E) v(E) f(E,T)\,dE$$
$$f(E,T) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\frac{\sqrt{E}}{T^{3/2}}e^{-E/T}$$
D-T reactivity peaks at T~20 keV with ⟨σv⟩~10⁻²² m³/s. D-D is ~100× lower. p-¹¹B requires T~300 keV with ⟨σv⟩~10⁻²⁴ m³/s.
