Penrose–Carter Diagrams

Conformal compactification maps all of spacetime (including infinities) into a finite diamond, preserving causal structure. Null rays always travel at 45°.
  • r_s = 2GM/c² — Schwarzschild radius
  • T² − X² = (1 − r/r_s)e^{r/r_s} — Kruskal
  • ℋ = ∂J⁻(𝒥⁺) — event horizon (teleological)
KRUSKAL MAXIMAL EXTENSION
Regions I (exterior), II (BH interior), III (white hole), IV (other exterior). Physical collapse uses only I+II with a star worldline — no white hole.
CAUSAL BOUNDARIES

Kerr–Newman (1965)

Most general asymptotically flat electrovacuum BH (Israel–Carter–Robinson uniqueness in 4D). Three hairs: mass, spin, charge.
  • Δ = r² − 2Mr + a² + Q²
  • r± = M ± √(M²−a²−Q²)
  • Φ_H = Q r₊/(r₊²+a²) — horizon potential
  • κ = (r₊−r₋)/(2(r₊²+a²))
EXTREMALITY
M² = a²+Q² ⇒ κ=0, coincident horizons. Cosmic censorship forbids overcharging/overspinning for classical matter (Wald; Sorce–Wald).
GYROMAGNETIC RATIO
g=2 for KN — same as Dirac electron. Carter: KN is type-D, separable geodesics + Maxwell.
ASTROPHYSICS
Astrophysical BHs are nearly neutral (selective accretion discharges Q). KN remains the exact electrovacuum baseline for charged accretion and minicharged DM searches.

Membrane Paradigm

External observers can replace the BH interior by a fictitious membrane at the stretched horizon with electrical resistivity, shear viscosity, and negative bulk viscosity (Damour; Thorne–Price–Macdonald).

  • σ_H^{ab} ↔ fluid stress
