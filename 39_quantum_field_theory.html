LSZ Reduction & S-matrix

LSZ formula:
$\langle f|S|i\rangle = \prod_j \left(\frac{i}{p_j^2-m^2}\right)\cdot\widetilde{G}^{(n)}(p_1,\ldots)$

Amputate external propagators and go on-shell.
Steps:
1. Compute full n-point Green's function
   $G^{(n)}(x_1,\ldots,x_n) = \langle 0|T\{\hat\phi(x_1)\cdots\hat\phi(x_n)\}|0\rangle$

2. Fourier transform to momentum space

3. Identify on-shell poles $p^2\to m^2$ in each leg

4. Multiply by $(p^2-m^2)/iZ^{1/2}$ per external leg

5. The residue is $\langle f|S|i\rangle$ (the amplitude $\mathcal{M}$)
On-shell pole:
$\tilde\Delta(p) = \frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i\epsilon}\xrightarrow{p^2\to m^2}\frac{iZ}{p^2-m^2}$

The residue $Z$ is the field-strength renormalization.
2→2 scattering:
$|\mathcal{M}|^2$ gives the differential cross section
$\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = \frac{|\mathcal{M}|^2}{64\pi^2 s}$

Mandelstam variables: $s=(p_1+p_2)^2$, $t=(p_1-p_3)^2$, $u=(p_1-p_4)^2$, $s+t+u=4m^2$.

$V_{\rm eff}(\phi) = V_{\rm cl} + \hbar\,V_{1\text{-loop}}$

Canonical Quantization / CCR

Equal-time algebra on a Cauchy slice Σ:
$[\hat\phi(\mathbf{x}),\hat\pi(\mathbf{y})] = i\,\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})$
$[\hat\phi,\hat\phi]=[\hat\pi,\hat\pi]=0$
Mode expansion:
$\hat\phi(x)=\!\int\!\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3\sqrt{2\omega_k}}\!\left(a_k e^{-ikx}+a_k^\dagger e^{ikx}\right)$
$\omega_k=\sqrt{\mathbf{k}^2+m^2}$. Each $k$ is a harmonic oscillator: $[a_k,a^\dagger_{k'}]=\delta_{kk'}$.
Pauli–Jordan function:
$i\Delta(x-y)=[\phi(x),\phi(y)]$ vanishes outside the light cone → microcausality.

$\Delta(x)=\int\!\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{\sin(\omega_k t)}{\omega_k}e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x}}$

Wick Theorem & Contractions

Free-field time-ordered correlators reduce to complete contractions:
$\langle 0|T\{\phi_1\cdots\phi_n\}|0\rangle = \sum_{\text{pairings}}\prod\Delta_F(x_i-x_j)$
Wick product: $:\phi_1\cdots\phi_n:$ is the fully normal-ordered product; all contractions removed.

Number of perfect matchings for $2m$ fields: $(2m-1)!! = (2m)!/(2^m m!)$.
Feynman propagator:
$\Delta_F(x)=\langle 0|T\{\phi(x)\phi(0)\}|0\rangle = \int\!\frac{d^4k}{(2\pi)^4}\frac{i\,e^{-ikx}}{k^2-m^2+i\epsilon}$
Connected correlators = only fully connected pairings. Vacuum bubbles cancel in $\langle O\rangle_c$ / LSZ.

Dyson–Schwinger Equations

Functional identity from $\int\!\mathcal{D}\phi\,\frac{\delta}{\delta\phi}e^{iS[\phi]+iJ\phi}=0$:
$\left\langle\frac{\delta S}{\delta\phi(x)}\right\rangle_J + J(x) = 0$
In terms of the 1PI effective action $\Gamma[\varphi]$:
$\frac{\delta\Gamma}{\delta\varphi(x)} = J(x)$ with $\varphi=\langle\phi\rangle_J$. Differentiating yields an infinite tower for $n$-point vertices.
Self-energy (φ⁴ sketch):
$\Sigma = \text{tadpole} + \text{sunset} + \cdots$ with full propagators $G=i/(p^2-m^2-\Sigma)$ and dressed vertices — non-perturbative resummation.

EFT Power Counting

Local EFT Lagrangian as operator expansion:
$\mathcal{L}_{\rm EFT} = \sum_i \frac{c_i}{\Lambda^{\Delta_i-d}}\,\mathcal{O}_i$
Engineering dimension: $[\phi]=(d-2)/2$, $[\partial]=1$. Relevant $\Delta<d$, marginal $\Delta=d$, irrelevant $\Delta>d$.

At energy $E$, irrelevant operators contribute $(E/\Lambda)^{\Delta-d}$.
Matching: integrate out heavy modes at scale $M\sim\Lambda$; Wilson coefficients $c_i(\mu)$ run via RG below $\Lambda$. Tree/loop matching fixes $c_i$ order by order in $1/\Lambda$.
Frontier: NDA (naive dimensional analysis), soft-collinear EFT, chiral PT $p/\Lambda_\chi$, and positivity bounds on $c_i$ from analyticity + unitarity.

BRST Cohomology (QFT angle)

Gauge-fixed + ghost extended action admits a nilpotent BRST charge $Q_B$:
$Q_B^2 = 0$
Physical Hilbert space:
$\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys} = \ker Q_B / \mathrm{im}\,Q_B = H(Q_B)$ (cohomology).
States of the form $Q_B|\chi\rangle$ have zero norm and decouple.
Ghost number grading: $Q_B$ raises ghost number by 1. Local observables are BRST-closed modulo BRST-exact operators — the classical BRST cohomology of the gauge theory.
Frontier: BV/BRST antifield formalism, renormalization of gauge theories via cohomological descent, holographic BRST in AdS/CFT.
