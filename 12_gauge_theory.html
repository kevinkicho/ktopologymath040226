SU(3) Color
Confinement: color flux lines collapse into a flux tube of constant tension $\sigma\approx 1$ GeV/fm. Energy grows linearly — quarks can never be isolated.

Gluon self-coupling: unlike photons, gluons carry color charge and interact with each other — causing the anti-screening that produces asymptotic freedom.

Lattice QCD confirms confinement numerically — the plaquette action reproduces the hadron spectrum at the 1–5% level. Quark-gluon plasma deconfines above $T_c\approx 150$ MeV.
Topology
Instanton: a self-dual field configuration in Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^4$ with winding number $Q=1$. It represents a tunneling event between topologically distinct vacua $|n\rangle\to|n{+}1\rangle$.

Strong CP problem: the $\theta$-term $\frac{\theta g^2}{32\pi^2}G\tilde{G}$ is CP-violating, yet experiments give $\theta < 10^{-10}$. Peccei-Quinn axions explain why.

Higgs Potential

Effective Action
Effective action: $\Gamma[\phi] = W[J] - \int J\phi$ is the Legendre transform of the generating functional. Its derivative gives the quantum equation of motion: $\delta\Gamma/\delta\phi = -J$. 1PI diagrams are the building blocks.

1PI = One-Particle-Irreducible
Diagrams that don't fall apart by cutting one line. The effective action is the sum of all 1PI diagrams.
S-Matrix
S-matrix: $\langle f|S|i\rangle = \delta_{fi} + i(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p_f-p_i)\mathcal{M}$. Unitarity $S^\dagger S = 1$ ensures probability conservation. The optical theorem relates forward scattering to total cross section.

Gauge Choice
Gauge-fixing: The path integral over gauge orbits is ill-defined. Adding $-\frac{1}{2\xi}(\partial^\mu A_\mu)^2$ breaks gauge symmetry and gives a well-defined propagator. Physical results are $\xi$-independent.
