Conformal (Penrose) Diagram

Compresses infinite spacetime into a finite diamond while preserving causal structure: null geodesics remain at 45°.

ds² → Ω² ds²   (causal angles fixed)

Region I: exterior. II: black-hole interior (all future nulls hit r=0). Horizons are the diagonals; singularity is the jagged top edge.

Mass M sets r_s = 2GM/c² and scales the visual horizon fraction.

Frontier: Carter–Penrose diagrams for Kerr, RN, and dynamical Vaidya; conformal compactification of asymptotically AdS (cf. module 62).

Energy Conditions

Classical singularity theorems assume matter obeys energy inequalities that force geodesic focusing.

NEC: ρ + p_i ≥ 0  ∀ i
WEC: ρ ≥ 0 and ρ + p_i ≥ 0
SEC: ρ + Σ p_i ≥ 0 and ρ + p_i ≥ 0
DEC: ρ ≥ |p_i|  (dominant)

Null energy condition (NEC) is the weakest used by Penrose 1965: R_μν k^μ k^ν ≥ 0 for null k.

Frontier: Quantum energy inequalities (QEI) replace pointwise NEC; ANEC holds for free QFTs on Minkowski (Faulkner et al.); semiclassical violations near horizons fuel Hawking flux.

Raychaudhuri Equation

dθ/dλ = −θ²/2 − σ² + ω² − R_μν k^μ k^ν

For irrotational (ω=0) null congruences under NEC (R_kk≥0): θ is driven to −∞ in finite affine parameter — a caustic.

Shear σ accelerates focusing; rotation ω can defocus (but ω=0 for hypersurface-orthogonal null generators).

Frontier: Quantum Raychaudhuri (QRE) with ⟨T_μν⟩; Bohmian / entanglement-entropy corrections (Das et al.); applications to holographic complexity.

Focusing Theorem

If θ(λ₀)=θ₀<0 and NEC holds with ω=0, then within affine length

λ_f − λ₀ ≤ 2 / |θ₀|

the expansion reaches −∞ (unless the geodesic ends earlier). This is the engine behind both Penrose and Hawking singularity theorems.

Intuition: negative expansion means cross-section area decreases; gravity can only make it worse under NEC.

Frontier: Average null energy condition (ANEC) suffices for many focusing results; quantum focusing conjecture (QFC) of Bousso et al. — proven in free QFT on Minkowski, open in curved spacetime.

Trapped Surfaces

A closed 2-surface is trapped when both outgoing and ingoing null expansions are negative: θ_out < 0 and θ_in < 0.

Outside the horizon θ_out > 0 (expanding), θ_in < 0. Inside, both converge — the geometric hallmark that collapse has gone past the point of no return.

Existence of a trapped surface is the key hypothesis of Penrose's 1965 theorem (not the event horizon itself, which is teleological).

Frontier: Marginally outer trapped surfaces (MOTS) and dynamical horizons in numerical GR; apparent horizon finders; quantum trapped surfaces in semiclassical gravity.
Theorem status: —

Penrose 1965 Incompleteness

Theorem (Penrose): If (M,g) is globally hyperbolic, satisfies NEC, and contains a closed trapped surface, then it is future null geodesically incomplete.

trapped + NEC + causality → incompleteness

"Singularity" here means geodesic incompleteness — not necessarily curvature blow-up (though that typically follows under extra assumptions).

Nobel Prize in Physics 2020 for this insight: collapse to a black hole is generic, not fine-tuned.

Frontier: Low-regularity singularity theorems (Kunzinger–Steinbauer et al.); trapped surfaces in dynamical spacetimes; incompleteness without global hyperbolicity via trapped surfaces + noncompact Cauchy surfaces.

Gravitational Collapse

As a massive star contracts past r_s = 2GM/c², an event horizon forms and a trapped surface is born. Penrose's theorem then guarantees incompleteness at r=0.

Light emitted from inside cannot escape; both families of null normals converge.

Frontier: Critical collapse (Choptuik scaling); naked singularities in fine-tuned dust (Tolman–Bondi); numerical GR of binary merger ringdown (cf. module 35 for BH exterior physics).
Past incompleteness: —

Hawking–Penrose Cosmology

Hawking's cosmological singularity theorem: under SEC (or suitable average energy conditions), global hyperbolicity, and a compact Cauchy surface with positive mean expansion (or a closed trapped surface in time-reversed form), spacetime is past incomplete — the Big Bang is not an artifact of FLRW symmetry.

SEC + expansion + causality → past incompleteness

Inflation can violate SEC temporarily (p ≈ −ρ), reopening the question of past completeness (Borde–Guth–Vilenkin theorem addresses expanding inflating spacetimes).

Frontier: BGV theorem (past incompleteness of inflation); quantum cosmology bounce scenarios; Hartle–Hawking no-boundary vs Vilenkin tunneling.

Cosmic Censorship

Weak (WCCC, Penrose): Singularities from generic collapse are hidden behind event horizons — no naked singularities visible from ℐ⁺.

Strong (SCCC): The maximal Cauchy development of generic initial data is locally inextendible as a C⁰ metric (or suitable class) — observers cannot cross Cauchy horizons into non-unique futures.

extremal: a² + Q² = M²   (naked if >)
Frontier: Christodoulou's proof of WCCC for spherical scalar collapse; Dafermos–Luk on Kerr Cauchy horizon stability (C⁰ extendible, C² unstable); overcharging/overspinning thought experiments with backreaction.

Cauchy Horizons & Mass Inflation

In RN and Kerr interiors, the inner (Cauchy) horizon is a null surface beyond which predictability fails. Poisson–Israel mass inflation: blue-shifted radiation makes the Misner–Sharp mass diverge as v→∞ along the Cauchy horizon.

m(v) ∼ e^{κ_- v} · (Price tail) → ∞

This produces a weak null singularity: curvature blows up but tidal forces may remain integrable (Ori). Supports SCCC in the C² sense while allowing C⁰ extensions in some models.

Frontier: Nonlinear stability of Kerr Cauchy horizon (Dafermos–Luk); charged Vaidya models; quantum stress-tensor near CH; holographic duals of mass inflation.

BKL / Mixmaster Chaos

Belinski–Khalatnikov–Lifshitz: near a spacelike singularity, spatial points decouple and each evolves as a homogeneous Bianchi IX cosmology — the Mixmaster universe.

ds² = −dt² + Σ a_i(t)² (σ^i)²

Scale factors undergo infinite Kasner epochs separated by bounces off curvature walls — a chaotic billiard on the hyperbolic plane (Misner, Chitre, Damour–Henneaux).

Frontier: Cosmological billiards in supergravity / M-theory; U-duality Weyl chambers; numerical GR confirmation of BKL in Gowdy and generic asymmetric collapse.

Quantum Gravity Resolutions (lite)

Classical theorems assume continuum Lorentzian geometry + energy conditions. Quantum gravity may evade incompleteness by:

  • LQG / polymer: bounce at ρ∼ρ_Pl; effective NEC violation
  • Strings / fuzzballs: horizon-scale microstructure, no interior singularity
  • Asymptotic safety: UV fixed point softens curvature
  • Regular metrics: Hayward / Bardeen cores with de Sitter interior
