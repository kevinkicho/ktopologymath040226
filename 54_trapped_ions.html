Cat State
Red = negative Wigner values — a purely quantum signature. Decoherence kills the fringes, producing a classical statistical mixture.
Wigner Function
$$W(\alpha) = \frac{2}{\pi}\text{Tr}[\hat{\rho}\,\hat{D}(\alpha)\hat{P}\hat{D}^\dagger(\alpha)]$$
Negative W values are a definitive marker of non-classical states. The cat state $|\alpha\rangle + |-\alpha\rangle$ shows interference fringes that decohere exponentially fast in a real environment.

Stable?—
The Mathieu equation $\ddot{u} + (a-2q\cos 2\xi)u=0$ governs ion motion. Stability requires $0\le a\le 0.237$ and $q\le 0.908$ in the first zone.
Mathieu Equation
$$\frac{d^2u}{d\xi^2} + (a-2q\cos 2\xi)u = 0$$
$$\beta \approx \sqrt{a + \frac{q^2}{2}}$$
$$\omega_{sec} = \beta\frac{\Omega}{2}$$
The characteristic exponent $\beta$ determines the secular frequency. For $a=0$, $\beta \approx q/\sqrt{2}$. Instability occurs when $\beta$ is an integer.
