Ehresmann connection (pure bundle geometry):
Horizontal distribution $H\subset TP$ with $TP=H\oplus V$, $V_p=\ker(d\pi_p)\cong\mathfrak{g}$, and $H_{pg}=(R_g)_*H_p$ (equivariance). Equivalent to connection form $\omega:TP\to\mathfrak{g}$ with $\omega(X^\#)=X$, $\omega|_H=0$, $R_g^*\omega=\mathrm{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\circ\omega$.

Parallel transport: for $\gamma:[0,1]\to M$, unique horizontal lift $\tilde\gamma$ with $\tilde\gamma(0)=p$; $\mathrm{PT}_\gamma(p)=\tilde\gamma(1)$. Holonomy = failure of $\mathrm{PT}$ around loops; curvature = infinitesimal non-integrability of $H$ (Frobenius).

Vs metric connections: no Riemannian structure required here — Levi-Civita is a special reduction on the frame bundle (→ Reduction tab / module 14).
Frontier: Lie algebroid / Courant connections; generalized geometry on $TM\oplus T^*M$ as bundle data beyond classical principal bundles.
Gauge theory: connection $A$ on principal $G$-bundle. Gauge transformation $g: P \to G$ equivariant. Field strength $F = dA + A\wedge A$. Instantons = self-dual connections on $S^4$.

Gauge theory as bundle geometry:
Connection $A$ on principal $G$-bundle $P\to M$; gauge group $\mathcal{G}=\mathrm{Aut}(P)$ acts by $A\mapsto gAg^{-1}+g\,dg^{-1}$, $F\mapsto gFg^{-1}$. Observables are $\mathcal{G}$-invariant (Wilson loops = holonomy characters).

Moduli (geometry, not full QFT):
$\mathcal{M}_k=\{A:F=\star F\}/\mathcal{G}$, $\dim\mathcal{M}_k=8k$ framed $SU(2)$ on $S^4$ (or $4k$ unframed conventions). ADHM = linear-algebra construction of all $k$-instantons.
